Before ordering, confirm display and touch drivers, board dimensions, battery connector and polarity, permitted cell voltage/capacity, charging and protection circuit, power-off/wake behavior and USB-C access. Manufacturer documentation was not accessible during this initial review; do not treat earlier battery estimates or connector assumptions as verified.

Candidate BOM: integrated display/controller, compatible protected rechargeable battery, USB cable, PETG enclosure, removable suction-lock mounting system, fasteners. No battery purchase until compatibility is verified.

Measure physical parts before final printing. Keep battery clear of fasteners and heat; test mount with inert equivalent weight and provide a secondary retention strategy if needed.
